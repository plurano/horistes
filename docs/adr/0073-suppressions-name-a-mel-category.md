# ADR-0073: Suppressions name a MEL category and expiry

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-11-09
- **Authors**: Lusoris

## Context

Some linter configurations enforced a flat 90-day maximum duration
([ADR-0010](0010-one-longest-time-for-every-exception.md)), while inline comment suppressions
in source files lacked uniform expiration dates and category metadata. Additionally, lines
generated by the Praetor engine (`praetorctl compile-context` or `adopt`) in markdown and
hook files cannot be modified directly in this repository without causing context drift.

## Decision

- Every suppression in this repository, whether an inline comment directive or configuration
  exception, states an expiry date, a fleet MEL category (`A`, `B`, `C`, or `D`), and a
  non-empty reason containing at least one letter and no commas.
- Each category fixes an interval: `A` states it in the reason as `interval N days` (1 <= N <= 90),
  `B` is 3 days, `C` 10 days and `D` 120 days.
- An `exceptions.yaml` entry expires exactly one interval after its `entered` day, since the fleet
  MEL rule sets the expiry from the entry date; a shorter time takes category `A` with its own
  interval, and a fix before the expiry removes the entry. Every other suppression (inline
  directives, accepted findings in `.github/zizmor.yml`, `.github/actionlint.yaml` and `ruff.toml`,
  and dated hosts in `hosts.toml`) names no entered day, so its expiry lies at most one interval
  after the day of the check.
- One reader, `tools/exceptions.py check` (`make check-exceptions`), judges all suppressions across
  all tracked files, while dated host exceptions in `hosts.toml` are checked by `tools/hosts.py check`.
- Per-category intervals replace the flat 90-day cap of ADR-0010, superseding it.
- A bandit suppression requires a second `#` before the expiry
  (`# nosec B602 # expires 2027-03-09 category D: reason`): bandit reads test ids only up to
  the next `#`, so the words after it raise no warning; bandit prints one warning per word
  for the form without it (measured with bandit 1.9.4), and the reader refuses that form.
- Inline directives in files must match the fleet format, or be covered by an entry in
  `exceptions.yaml` ([ADR-0007](0007-one-own-exception-list.md)).
- REUSE ignore markers are prohibited in source files and stay refused by `tools/exceptions.py`;
  caller templates keep licence field lines from being read as tags via an empty template
  note (`# SPDX-<%/* */%>...`).
- Eleven engine-owned lines are tracked in `exceptions.yaml` under issue `cordanaLLM/praetor#1878`
  with category `C` until emitted by an updated engine release.
- A ratchet baseline in `suppression-baseline.json` records the count of directive lines by kind
  and fails if any count grows or shrinks without updating the baseline.
- Unit, negative, and boundary tests in `tests/test_exceptions.py` and `tests/test_suppressions.py`
  verify suppression rules, expiration limits, and ratchet enforcement.

## Consequences

- **Positive**: All suppressions throughout the repository follow one verifiable fleet standard
  with consistent categorization and expiration.
- **Negative**: Suppressions must be renewed or resolved according to their MEL intervals.
- **Supersedes**: Supersedes the flat 90-day longest time of ADR-0010 and extends ADR-0007's
  exception list to inline directives.
